# NillaNinja Business Dojo
## Full Business Snapshot Assessment Blueprint

## Version 0.1 Target

The complete Business Snapshot will contain 42 questions:

- 7 assessment categories
- 6 questions per category
- Approximately 10–15 minutes to complete
- Plain-English questions suitable for owner-operated businesses with 1–20 employees
- One answer per screen
- Standard weighted scoring and recommendation logic

## Question Design Rules

Each question should:

- Evaluate one clear business practice.
- Avoid technical or corporate language.
- Be understandable without outside assistance.
- Apply broadly across service, retail, trade, creative, and local businesses.
- Include help text with realistic examples.
- Use the shared five-answer scale.
- Include a weight from 1–4.
- Connect to one or more recommendation keys.
- Allow “Not applicable” when the practice may not fit every business.

Higher weights should be reserved for practices involving:

- Financial or operational disruption
- Security or permanent data loss
- Customer trust
- Business continuity
- Heavy owner dependency

## Category 1 — Operations

Purpose:

Evaluate how consistently the business plans, performs, tracks, and improves recurring work.

Questions:

1. Written procedures
2. Scheduling process
3. Task and deadline tracking
4. Estimate, proposal, or quote consistency
5. Inventory or supply monitoring
6. Process review and improvement

## Category 2 — Customer Experience

Purpose:

Evaluate how reliably the business communicates with, supports, and learns from customers.

Questions:

1. Customer follow-up
2. Response-time expectations
3. Customer information organization
4. Complaint and issue handling
5. Feedback collection
6. Repeat-customer or retention process

## Category 3 — Sales and Marketing

Purpose:

Evaluate how the business attracts, tracks, follows up with, and converts potential customers.

Questions:

1. Lead tracking
2. Consistent business message
3. Website or online presence accuracy
4. Marketing activity tracking
5. Referral process
6. Sales follow-up responsibility

## Category 4 — Technology and Workflow

Purpose:

Evaluate whether tools, files, and information support efficient daily work.

Questions:

1. Duplicate data entry
2. Business file organization
3. Tool and software usefulness
4. Mobile access to important information
5. Repetitive-task automation
6. Outdated or disconnected systems

## Category 5 — Team and Responsibility

Purpose:

Evaluate whether responsibilities, information, and decisions are clear enough for the business to operate consistently.

Questions:

1. Owner dependency
2. Clear employee or contractor responsibilities
3. Training and onboarding
4. Access to needed information
5. Routine decision authority
6. Performance and accountability check-ins

## Category 6 — Accessibility and Inclusion

Purpose:

Evaluate whether customers with different needs can access information, communicate, and complete important actions.

Questions:

1. Customer contact options
2. Website readability and usability
3. Accessible digital content
4. Alternative ways to complete important actions
5. Clear customer instructions
6. Accommodation request process

## Category 7 — Security and Continuity

Purpose:

Evaluate how well the business protects access, information, and its ability to recover from disruption.

Questions:

1. Password sharing
2. Data backups
3. Multi-factor authentication
4. Former employee or contractor access removal
5. Device and software updates
6. Business interruption and recovery plan

## Weight Guidance

Weight 4 — Critical:

- Data backups
- Password and account security
- Business interruption recovery
- Practices where failure could stop operations or cause permanent loss

Weight 3 — Important:

- Written procedures
- Owner dependency
- Customer issue handling
- Lead follow-up
- Responsibility and access clarity

Weight 2 — Standard:

- Scheduling
- File organization
- Marketing consistency
- Customer communication options
- Routine workflow improvements

Weight 1 — Supporting:

- Helpful maturity practices with lower immediate risk

## Recommendation Behavior

- “Yes, consistently” should create a possible strength.
- “Somewhat established” should normally affect scoring but not trigger a Version 0.1 recommendation.
- “Informal or inconsistent” should trigger related recommendations.
- “Not currently in place” should trigger related recommendations.
- “Not applicable” should be excluded from scoring and recommendations.
- Duplicate recommendation keys should be combined.
- The report should show no more than six prioritized recommendations.
- Critical security and continuity recommendations should remain visible when triggered.
