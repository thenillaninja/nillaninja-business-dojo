# Business Snapshot Assessment Model

## Version

Assessment Model: 0.1  
Status: Planning

## Purpose

The Business Snapshot is a guided assessment for owner-operated and small businesses with approximately 1–20 employees.

Its purpose is to identify:

- Operational strengths
- Inconsistent or undocumented processes
- Owner dependency
- Customer experience problems
- Repetitive manual work
- Technology gaps
- Accessibility opportunities
- Security and continuity risks
- Practical areas for improvement

The assessment must produce useful recommendations rather than simply assigning a score.

## Assessment Categories

### 1. Business Foundation

Purpose:

Understand the basic structure, size, priorities, and operating context of the business.

Topics:

- Business type
- Years in operation
- Number of employees
- Products or services
- Customer types
- Current priorities
- Primary business challenges

### 2. Operations

Purpose:

Identify whether recurring work is organized, documented, and completed consistently.

Topics:

- Recurring processes
- Written procedures
- Scheduling
- Task consistency
- Inventory or material tracking
- Daily bottlenecks
- Owner involvement

### 3. Customer Experience

Purpose:

Review how the business communicates with customers before, during, and after a sale or service.

Topics:

- Inquiry response
- Estimates and proposals
- Customer updates
- Complaint handling
- Follow-up
- Repeat business
- Contact options

### 4. Sales and Marketing

Purpose:

Determine how consistently the business attracts, tracks, and follows up with potential customers.

Topics:

- Lead sources
- Lead tracking
- Website presence
- Social media
- Customer reviews
- Marketing consistency
- Sales follow-up

### 5. Technology and Workflow

Purpose:

Identify repetitive work, disconnected tools, duplicate entry, and opportunities to simplify daily operations.

Topics:

- Current software
- Manual processes
- Duplicate data entry
- File organization
- Tool usability
- Automation opportunities
- AI suitability

### 6. Team and Responsibility

Purpose:

Identify unclear responsibilities, training gaps, communication problems, and excessive reliance on the owner.

Topics:

- Role clarity
- Employee training
- Task ownership
- Internal communication
- Delegation
- Knowledge dependency
- Owner workload

### 7. Accessibility and Inclusion

Purpose:

Review whether customers can understand, access, and complete important interactions with the business.

Topics:

- Website usability
- Clear communication
- Contact choices
- Accessible forms
- Alternative service methods
- Digital accessibility awareness
- Customer inclusion

### 8. Security and Continuity

Purpose:

Identify basic risks that could interrupt operations or expose important business information.

Topics:

- Password practices
- Account access
- Backups
- Shared credentials
- Customer information
- Business continuity
- Key-person dependency

## Assessment Design Rules

- Use plain English.
- Ask one clear question at a time.
- Avoid technical terminology unless it is explained.
- Explain why a question is being asked when necessary.
- Use descriptive question identifiers.
- Allow a Not Applicable response when appropriate.
- Do not punish a business for its size.
- Score practices, not access to expensive software.
- Recognize effective manual systems when they work consistently.
- Avoid recommending AI unless it solves a real problem.
- Keep the full assessment within approximately 10–15 minutes.

## Initial Development Dataset

Before building the full assessment, create a test dataset containing approximately 10 questions.

The test questions should cover:

- Written procedures
- Scheduling
- Customer follow-up
- Lead tracking
- Duplicate data entry
- File organization
- Owner dependency
- Website contact options
- Password sharing
- Data backups

The test dataset must be sufficient to validate:

- Question rendering
- Answer storage
- Weighted scoring
- Recommendation triggers
- Category findings
- Report generation
